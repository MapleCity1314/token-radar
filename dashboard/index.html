MODEL

Nonlinear family and risk model

Implied value = TVL × 0.25 × category factor × growth factor × downside factor × age factor. Growth is clamped to -60%…120%, category factor uses family medians, positive growth is bounded by tanh, negative growth receives a separate downside deduction, and age follows a diminishing-return curve.

Family buckets are lending, dex, derivatives, restaking, bridge, vault, yield, and general. The dashboard recomputes the formula from every row’s stored inputs before showing detail.
